What Is Dyslexia?

Signs a child may need support

Dyslexia looks different in every child. These are some of the common signs parents and teachers may notice.

🔤

Reading & spelling

Slow, effortful reading, frequent guessing, and spelling that varies from one attempt to the next.

🔁

Letters & sounds

Difficulty linking letters to sounds, or reversing similar letters and numbers.

🧩

Memory & sequence

Trouble remembering instructions, days of the week, or the order of steps.

💬

Word finding

Knowing an answer but struggling to find or pronounce the right word.

⏱️

Organisation

Losing track of time, belongings, or where they are on a page.

✨

Strengths too

Many dyslexic children are creative, curious problem-solvers who thrive with the right approach.

Our Services

How we teach

Our teaching is language-based, multi-sensory, structured, sequential, cumulative, cognitive and flexible — using the Orton-Gillingham approach.

👤

One-on-one tutoring

Individual sessions focused on the underlying learning skills each child needs, at a pace that suits them.

🖐️

Multi-sensory teaching

Seeing, hearing, saying and touching together, so new skills stick through more than one pathway.

📝

Homework guidance

Support with schoolwork in both Bahasa Malaysia and English to keep children on track in class.
